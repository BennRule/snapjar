Questions

Do you print photos?

Not yet. Snapjar is a digital booth: every guest gets their photos on their phone straight away, and you get the whole set afterwards. Printed strips are on the way.

How much space do you need?

About 3m × 3m, indoors or under cover, with a standard plug socket within 5m.

What events do you do?

Birthdays, christenings, weddings, proms, school discos, work dos, Christmas parties and anything else that deserves photos.

When do you arrive?

An hour before your booth time starts, so we're ready before guests arrive. Please check your venue lets us in that early.

How do guests get their photos?

They scan a code on the screen or type in their number or email, and the photos arrive within seconds. Good venue wifi or phone signal helps, and we bring a backup connection.

Are you insured?

Yes. We carry public liability insurance and our kit is PAT tested. We can send certificates to your venue.

Can we change our date?

Yes, if the new date is free and you give us at least 14 days' notice. Your deposit moves with you.

Booking terms

The short version, so there are no surprises.

  1. Deposit. A £50 non-refundable deposit holds your date and comes off your total.
  2. Balance. The rest is due 7 days before your event. Bookings made within 7 days are paid in full when booking.
  3. Cancelling. Cancel more than 14 days before and you lose only the deposit. Within 14 days, the full price is due.
  4. Changing your date. Free with at least 14 days' notice, if the new date is available.
  5. Your venue. You confirm the venue allows a photo booth and lets us in an hour before booth time. We need a 3m × 3m space under cover and a working plug socket within 5m.
  6. Booth time. Booth time is the hours you book, from the agreed start. If the party runs late, extra time is £35 an hour if we can stay.
  7. Damage. You're responsible for damage to the kit caused by guests, apart from normal wear and tear.
  8. Behaviour. Our host can pause the booth if anyone's behaviour puts people or the kit at risk.
  9. Photos. You and your guests own your photos. We'll only share them on our website or social media with your permission, and never photos of children.
  10. If we can't make it. In the unlikely event we have to cancel, you get every penny back.
